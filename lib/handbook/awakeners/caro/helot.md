Helot is an offensive support who multiplies the damage of Strikes. Her exalt provides a powerful buff for strike-based DPS, such as [Mouchette](/handbook/awakeners/mouchette/) and [GHelot](/handbook/awakeners/ghelot/).

At high investment, she can also be used as a DPS herself. However, Helot’s DPS build is rather weak, and not recommended unless you really like her.

## How to Play

**Support:** Use Helot’s exalt for a damage buff for your DPS. Her cards are essentially worthless and can be sold.

**DPS:** Use Helot’s basic Strike and Defense for crit buffs. Against mobs, use Lasting Loathe to deal AOE damage. Against single targets and bosses, rouse Helot, then stall as long as possible so that you can build up huge Shattering Strikes.

## Teambuilding

**Support:** Helot is most commonly used as a damage buffer for [Mouchette](/handbook/awakeners/mouchette/) and [GHelot](/handbook/awakeners/ghelot/).

**DPS:** If you want to use Helot as a DPS, her low damage and long setup time mean that she needs powerful supports to carry her.

[Arachne](/handbook/awakeners/arachne/) provides tons of [DMG Amplification](/handbook/team#dmg-amplification-base-dmg) and a Final DMG bonus with her pursuits, giving Helot the damage she needs to clear high-difficulty content.

Teammates that inflict corrosion, like [Saya](/handbook/awakeners/saya/) and [Vivian](/handbook/awakeners/vivian/), are enormously helpful for clearing mobs, as Helot's only AOE damage option, Lasting Loathe, is quite weak.

Generically powerful supports like [Thais](/handbook/awakeners/thais/), [Caraboo](/handbook/awakeners/caraboo/), [Clementine](/handbook/awakeners/clementine/), and [Horla](/handbook/awakeners/horla/) are all good teammates for DPS Helot.

Because Shattering Strike’s damage is based on Strikes in hand, DPS Helot benefits a lot from effects that raise your hand size, such as [Merciful Nurturing](https://skeydb.com/database/wheels/merciful-nurturing){target="_blank"} and [Drowning in Crimson](https://skeydb.com/database/wheels/drowning-in-crimson){target="_blank"}.

## High Investment

Helot’s OE and AA are damage multipliers that improve her DPS.

## Important Stats

:dmg-amp: [DMG Amplification](/handbook/team#dmg-amplification-base-dmg) boosts Helot’s exalt buff, making it useful for both support and DPS builds. For DPS Helot, it also represents a sizeable damage increase, since DMG amplification is multiplicative with the Base DMG scaling from her rouse.

:crit-dmg: Crit DMG and :crit-rate: Crit Rate are important for DPS Helot, but not required to build, because Helot can generate them by playing Strike and Defense or using embryos. The most effective build for DPS Helot is to build mostly DMG amplification on Helot, and have the rest of the team grant her crit buffs.

Base DMG bonuses are good for DPS Helot if you can get them from wheels or relics.
