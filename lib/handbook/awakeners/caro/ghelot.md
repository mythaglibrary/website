Helot: Catena (“GHelot”) is a risky but powerful DPS who drains her own HP to set up big combos.

## Crimson Shackles & Bloodthirsty Flail

GHelot’s gameplay revolves around her skill **Crimson Shackles**, and the powerful attack it transforms into, **Bloodthirsty Flail**.

Crimson Shackles is a weak attack that damages GHelot herself when played. However, if you play it enough times, it turns into **Bloodthirsty Flail**, a big hit that triggers bleed and heals GHelot’s missing HP.

Once you have a Flail in hand, you can copy it over and over with [The Lone Seed](https://skeydb.com/database/posses/the-lone-seed){target="_blank"}, the posse unlocked after beating Chapter 7. This lets GHelot deal massive damage to enemies while healing herself up at the same time.

GHelot’s E3 lets her start every fight with an Irregular Form in hand, which can be played to guarantee a Crimson Shackles in hand on turn 1. This makes her setup extremely consistent; I recommend pulling E3 for any serious GHelot player.

## How to Play

### Easy Enemies

If you're a new player doing low-level stages, and you don’t have [The Lone Seed](https://skeydb.com/database/posses/the-lone-seed){target="_blank"} unlocked, the most effective way to use GHelot is to spam her AOE attack, **Hatred Unleashed**. This will mop up most groups of enemies without sacrificing any HP.

This also works for any stage where you’re heavily overleveled and you just want to clear as fast as possible.

### Flail Combo

Against tougher enemies, you should rouse GHelot, then stall for as many turns as possible while building up Bloodthirsty Flails in hand.

GHelot’s rouse gives her a permanent stacking Base DMG multiplier each turn. GHelot will also deal more damage the more Flails she plays in one turn, because each Flail triggers the bleed inflicted by all previous Flails. This means that the most efficient strategy is to wait until you can knock out the entire enemy HP bar in one turn.

If you find yourself dying early on, you can play one or two Bloodthirsty Flails to restore your HP. You can also bring other defensive supports, such as [Celeste](/handbook/awakeners/celeste/), if you plan on using this strategy.

Once you're ready to burst, use GHelot’s exalt to add bonus bleed to every hit. Then play Strikes until you reach 100% chance to crit, and throw out Bloodthirsty Flails until you’ve inflicted enough bleed to kill.

If you have enough Flails, you can even trigger a boss’s second phase, then continue hitting them with Flails to stack bleed. By doing this, a highly invested GHelot can take out two HP bars in one turn.

## Teambuilding

GHelot is a [hypercarry](/handbook/team#hypercarry), and as such, should be accompanied with three supports to maximize her damage.

I suggest putting the covenant [Deus Ex Machina](/handbook/team#deus-ex-machina) on all three of her supports. This gives you enough arithmetica to rouse GHelot and play Crimson Shackles on turn 1. (Credit for this tactic goes to Aurora, the best GHelot player in EN.)

The single best support for GHelot is [Thais](/handbook/awakeners/thais/). Thais can copy Bloodthirsty Flail with her exalt, giving you free Flails to heal with, and extra Flails during your burst turn. She also provides a host of useful abilities like debuffs and double Devour triggers.

[Aigis](/handbook/awakeners/aigis/) is very useful for GHelot teams, because GHelot is most vulnerable in the first few turns when she’s sacrificing HP to set up her Flails. Aigis’s stun can buy an extra turn, which often makes the difference between dying and effortlessly clearing.

[Helot](/handbook/awakeners/helot/) is a strong support for GHelot, because Bloodthirsty Flail counts as a Strike, and gets a large damage buff from Helot’s exalt.

Ultra supports, like [Horla](/handbook/awakeners/horla/) and [Arachne](/handbook/awakeners/arachne/), give GHelot extra arithmetica and extra turns to set up. Arachne also provides huge [DMG amplification](/handbook/team#dmg-amplification-base-dmg) and Final DMG buffs with her pursuits, making her indispensable for GHelot teams in high-difficulty content.

[Celeste](/handbook/awakeners/celeste/) is a defensive support who may be helpful if you keep dying. Aequor also adds additional survivability with Tranquil Sea stance.

[Saya](/handbook/awakeners/saya/)’s corrosion is a powerful damage multiplier, but she’s hard to use with GHelot as she requires you to discard your hand.

## High Investment

GHelot’s OE gives a significant damage buff, and creates a Crimson Shackles in your hand, which is permanently added to your deck. This makes her OE useful both for setting up and for your burst turn.

Her AA causes one of the Bloodthirsty Flails in your hand to receive a temporary 50% damage buff. This is deceptively powerful, since it increases the bleed triggered by all future Bloodthirsty Flails that turn, and results in a good damage bonus.

## Important Stats

GHelot is one of the rare characters who can generate a lot of Crit Rate by herself. Her rouse also gives her a scaling Base DMG multiplier, which is multiplicative with [DMG amplification](/handbook/team#dmg-amplification-base-dmg) and Base DMG bonuses.

If you have the [April Tribute](/handbook/team#april-tribute) covenant on GHelot, you can just put one :crit-rate: Crit Rate wheel on her, and then invest in :crit-dmg: Crit DMG, :dmg-amp: DMG Amplification, and Base DMG multipliers (such as the effect of [Drowning in Crimson](https://skeydb.com/database/wheels/drowning-in-crimson){target="_blank"}).

Alternatively, you can run the [Steppenwolf](/handbook/team#steppenwolf) covenant on GHelot, and build just enough crit to reach 100% crit chance after one Strike and one embryo.
