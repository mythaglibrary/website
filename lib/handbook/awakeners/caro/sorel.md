Sorel is a combo DPS who sets up and then unleashes a high-damage exalt.

This character is a very needy diva. She can be likened to a machinegun that needs bullets to work. If you don't have enough embryos, or STR, or damage buffs, Sorel does nothing. However, if you set her up properly and give her everything she wants, she can deal millions of damage with a single exalt.

Because of how reliant Sorel is on other limited characters, I don't recommend that new players pull for her. However, if you really like her, you can invest into her team and see good results.

## How to Play

Use your other characters to generate a lot of STR and embryos in hand, then press Sorel’s exalt.

If you have leftover arithmetica after bursting, you can play Sorel's Strike and Elegance of Rose for more damage. Do this after Sorel exalts, so you benefit from the temporary STR generated.

## Teambuilding

The number one support for Sorel is [Pickman](/handbook/awakeners/pickman/). Pickman generates a lot of STR, embryos, and Exalt Final DMG, and is everything Sorel could want in a teammate.

[Thais](/handbook/awakeners/thais/) is also a very powerful support for Sorel, as her Scions of Purity count as two embryos when devoured. She also provides a host of useful abilities like weakness, vulnerable, and aliemus generation.

If you don’t have or can’t spare either of these characters, you can use other embryo generators, like [Aigis](/handbook/awakeners/aigis/), [Agrippa](/handbook/awakeners/agrippa/), or [Leigh](/handbook/awakeners/leigh/); and other supports for buffing her damage, like [Clementine](/handbook/awakeners/clementine/), [Saya](/handbook/awakeners/saya/), or [Vivian](/handbook/awakeners/vivian/).

Sorel works best in Aequor/Caro teams, since every hit of her exalt will be followed up by a tentacle attack in Raging Waves stance. Good Aequor teammates to bring include [GMurphy](/handbook/awakeners/gmurphy/), [Murphy](/handbook/awakeners/murphy/), and [Celeste](/handbook/awakeners/celeste/).

## High Investment

Sorel is a strange beast when it comes to investment, because most of her damage comes from her supports. If you want to boost your Sorel’s damage, the best thing to pull is more copies of [Pickman](/handbook/awakeners/pickman/).

If you already have AA Pickman, you can consider pulling for Sorel enlightens. Her OE is useful for retaining extra aliemus, and her AA is a large Final DMG bonus.
