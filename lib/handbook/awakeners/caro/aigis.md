Aigis is a [keyflare support](/handbook/team#the-almighty-keyflare-bot) who brings various powerful abilities to the team.

### Petrify

Aigis's unique ability is to **Petrify** enemies with her exalt. This effectively lets you take an extra turn in every fight.

(In other words: “Gain 5 arithmetica, draw 5 cards, grant 5 aliemus to all awakeners, generate embryo fusion and Crimson Furnace, dispel 1 stack of all debuffs, reset [Arithmetica Harmony](/handbook/storylines#arithmetica-harmony)...”)

Early on, you might not realize how powerful this is, since enemies do very little damage each turn. In endgame, when faced with thousands of incoming damage, and enemies that take multiple turns to kill, Aigis’s ability to buy time is godlike.

### Aigis Cheese

By Petrifying bosses at the right moment, Aigis can even delay their intention to rouse or change phases. This can result in a turn where the boss is no longer immune to damage, but hasn’t roused or changed phases yet, so you can kill the boss without ever entering its second phase.

“Aigis cheese” used to be effective against the multi-phase bosses at the end of story chapters, but most of these exploits have been patched out. It’s still useful against some normal bosses with annoying second phases, like “Lampshade” Aisha.

### Other Support Skills

If granting extra turns wasn’t enough, Aigis is also the best vulnerable applier in the game, and can maintain 100% vulnerable uptime on enemies with minimal effort.

She also generates a lot of keyflare with Decomposition, and at E3, can generate embryo fusion with her Strike and A Small Wish.

## How to Play

Use Aigis’s Devour effect to petrify enemies. Play her cards to get keyflare and embryo fusion.

Aigis’s exalt and A Small Wish give you easy access to vulnerable at any time.

If you need aliemus to petrify enemies, you can rouse Aigis.

## Teambuilding

Aigis fits into any team that wants keyflare and vulnerable application. In other words, basically every team.

She works best with glass cannon DPS that just need one turn of breathing room to set up, such as [GHelot](/handbook/awakeners/ghelot/), [Pollux](/handbook/awakeners/pollux/), or [Mouchette](/handbook/awakeners/mouchette/).

## High Investment

Aigis’s OE grants a +25% Final DMG buff, in case you weren’t getting enough value out of this free character.

## Important Stats

Aigis is a [keyflare support](/handbook/team#the-almighty-keyflare-bot) and should be built with :skey: Keyflare Regen.
