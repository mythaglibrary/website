Salvador is a tough defensive support who buffs your Crimson Furnace.

He is most notable for his 2-cost skill, **End of Suffering.** This is a very powerful offensive and defensive tool, which can make huge shields, or deal good AOE damage.

His exalt also provides consistent access to vulnerable, and he can inflict weakness, though at an expensive arithmetica cost.

## How to Play

Use Salvador’s cards to generate more Crimson Furnace, and play End of Suffering to shield at key moments. You can also rouse Salvador for a barrier if you need one.

If you run Salvador with [Thais](/handbook/awakeners/thais/) or [The Lone Seed](https://skeydb.com/database/posses/the-lone-seed){target="_blank"}, you can copy End of Suffering over and over. This gives you a giant shield every turn, and even lets Salvador function as a DPS.

## Teambuilding

Salvador is a flexible defensive support who fits into any team that needs help surviving.

At endgame, he's usually seen in [Vortice](/handbook/awakeners/vortice/) stall teams, helping the team survive long enough to grind out boss fights.

He also works extremely well with [Caraboo](/handbook/awakeners/caraboo/), because increasing your Max HP increases the Crimson Furnace generated each turn.

If you're running DPS Salvador, you can use [Horla](/handbook/awakeners/horla/)’s Grief to multiply the Crimson Furnace his cards generate. This directly accelerates his damage scaling.

## High Investment

Salvador’s OE and AA improve his damage when used as a DPS.

## Important Stats

:sigil-yield: Sigil Yield on Salvador increases the capacity of your Crimson Furnace. This is generally not worth it compared to effects that buff his shielding, like [Data is Flesh](https://skeydb.com/database/wheels/data-is-flesh){target="_blank"}, or effects that buff your Max HP, like [Gluttony](https://skeydb.com/database/wheels/gluttony){target="_blank"}. However, if you have Salvador’s SSR wheel [Tablet of Scriptures](https://skeydb.com/database/wheels/tablet-of-scriptures){target="_blank"}, it’s extra valuable because of the Sigil Yield main stat.
