Thais is the best embryo maker, an excellent keyflare and aliemus generator, a card copying machine, and an overall ridiculous support who does everything your team could possibly want.

Her Scions of Purity can trigger Devour twice and are game-changing for many Caro teams.

At E2 she is arguably the best support in the game. She isn't in every "Works Well With" section only because it would be redundant.
