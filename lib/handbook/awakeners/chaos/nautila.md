Nautila is a highly specific support who is mainly used for three things.

Firstly, she can multiply your current shield with her exalt. This is both a useful defensive tool, and a damage multiplier for shield-based DPS, like [Alva](/handbook/awakeners/alva/).

Secondly, her exalt deals Fixed DMG to all enemies based on your counter. This means that she can be a backup damage dealer for [counter teams](/handbook/team#counter).

Thirdly, at E3, her exalt also blocks incoming debuffs. This makes her useful against specific bosses that inflict debuffs, such as the Chapter 9 boss.

## How to Play

If Nautila is on your team, she’s probably there for her exalt. Just save her exalt and use it at the best time.

Nautila’s cards are mostly not useful. Short-Term Memory is decent if you have nothing else to spend arithmetica on; No Trespassing is not very useful and can safely be sold.

## Teambuilding

Nautila is most often seen in [Mono Chaos team](/handbook/team#mono-chaos-teams), which can quickly charge her exalt with Chaos Realm Mastery. For example, she can be put in a [Tawil](/handbook/awakeners/tawil/) and [Chaos 24](/handbook/awakeners/24/) team, boosting the defensive capabilities of the supports that are already there.

She can also be used with shield scaling DPS, like [Alva](/handbook/awakeners/alva/) or [Sanga](/handbook/awakeners/sanga/), since her exalt is effectively a damage buff for them.

Some [Daffodil](/handbook/awakeners/daffodil/) teams run Nautila as a way to convert the temporary counter from Thousand Mirage into AOE damage. However, after recent Daffodil buffs, she’s better at dealing with normal enemies, so Nautila isn’t really needed in the team anymore.

If your team is struggling against a boss that inflicts many debuffs, E3 Nautila can be brought in the 4th slot to counter the boss mechanics.

## High Investment

Nautila’s OE both generates more shield and inflicts more damage based on your counter, improving her niche support role.

## Important Stats

:realm-mastery: Realm Mastery gives a miniscule buff to the counter inflicted by Nautila’s exalt. This is so insignificant that it isn’t really worth building for.

However, if Nautila is in a [Mono Chaos team](/handbook/team#mono-chaos-teams), you want Realm Mastery on the team anyway for the aliemus, so it doesn’t hurt to put more RM wheels on her.
