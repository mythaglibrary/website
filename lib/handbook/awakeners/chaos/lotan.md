Lotan is a straightforward DPS who deals damage with strikes and her exalt.

Playing strikes reduces the cost of Blade of Defiance, her highest-damage card, and boosts the damage of her exalt over time.

As a basic strike DPS, Lotan is relatively weak. She deals less damage than [Alva](/handbook/awakeners/alva/), [Mouchette](/handbook/awakeners/mouchette/), or [GLotan](/handbook/awakeners/glotan/).

## High Investment

Lotan is most notable for her Over-Exaltation, which radically changes her playstyle.

Her OE deals damage equal to 15% of the enemy’s Max HP. It doesn’t matter how many millions of HP a boss has: if you OE eight times with Lotan, that HP bar is gone.

Endgame players usually ignore the rest of Lotan’s kit and use her as a “deal 15% Max HP” button. In this capacity, she’s a powerful support, and can even solo DPS against bosses that have Ancient Embers, or only one HP bar.

## How to Play

**Strike build:** Play Strikes and use Lotan’s exalt. Against bosses, use her OE instead.

**OE build:** Feed aliemus to Lotan and use her OE off cooldown.

## Teambuilding

**Strike build:** Strike Lotan works well with supports that can copy her cards, like [Thais](/handbook/awakeners/thais/) and [Hameln](/handbook/awakeners/hameln/), or buff them, like [Helot](/handbook/awakeners/helot/) and [Arachne](/handbook/awakeners/arachne/).

**OE build:** OE Lotan is usually run in two types of teams.

In a Mono Chaos team, Realm Mastery gives Lotan a lot of aliemus, while supports like [Tawil](/handbook/awakeners/tawil/) and [Doll](/handbook/awakeners/doll/) can stall for time.

In a Caro team with [Faint](/handbook/awakeners/faint/), you can abuse death resistance to charge Lotan's aliemus with [Burial Ground’s Sighs](/handbook/team#burial-grounds-sighs).

In both cases, [GDoll](/handbook/awakeners/gdoll/) can provide Lotan with aliemus. [GDoll](/handbook/awakeners/gdoll/) and [Tawil](/handbook/awakeners/tawil/) work well with a Max HP % damage strategy in general, since they both have Max HP % damage as well.

## Important Stats

Build :crit-dmg: Crit DMG and :crit-rate: Crit Rate if you want Lotan to deal damage with her cards.
