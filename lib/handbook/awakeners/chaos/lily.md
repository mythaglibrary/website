Lily is a simple but powerful tank who shields and heals for a lot. She gains **Endure** from taking damage, which her exalt converts into healing.

She’s also good at applying and triggering poison, and can function as a secondary DPS. Because she’s an offensive and defensive all-rounder, a built Lily can solo story stages by herself.

## How to Play

Play Lily’s Defense and Forbidden Swamp for shields. Use her exalt for more healing and shielding.

If the enemies have a lot of poison on them, Strike to Protect can be used as a finisher. Otherwise, you shouldn’t use it carelessly, as it will use up your Endure stacks.

## Teambuilding

Lily is a universal tank who fits into any team that needs help staying alive.

At endgame, she’s most commonly seen in dedicated poison teams, with teammates like [GDoll](/handbook/awakeners/gdoll/), [Xu](/handbook/awakeners/xu/), and [Nymphaea](/handbook/awakeners/nymphaea/).

Since she’s a Chaos character with a powerful exalt, which can be charged up with Mono Chaos Realm Mastery, she can support Mono Chaos carries like [Chaos 24](/handbook/awakeners/24/) and [OE Lotan](/handbook/awakeners/lotan/).

## High Investment

E3 is the recommended breakpoint for Lily, as it significantly improves her healing.

Her OE increases her Endure cap by 100%, literally doubling her healing for the fight. If you stall long enough to use it multiple times, it makes the team functionally immortal.

## Important Stats

:death-resistance: Death Resistance gives a big bonus to Lily’s shielding, and should be prioritized as much as possible.
