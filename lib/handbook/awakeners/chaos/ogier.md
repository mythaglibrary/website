Ogier is a shielder who also provides Temporary STR and a bit of vulnerable.

In most teams, Ogier is not very powerful. In a Mono Chaos team, he can exalt every turn because of the aliemus from Chaos Realm Mastery, and becomes a decent shielder and buffer.

## How to Play

Use Ogier’s exalt and Unstable Barrier for shields. Use Piercing Strike to inflict vulnerable.

## Teambuilding

Ogier is best used in Mono Chaos teams as a defensive support. For example, he can be a shielder for [Chaos 24](/handbook/awakeners/24/) or [OE Lotan](/handbook/awakeners/lotan/).

## High Investment

Ogier’s OE and AA boost his exalt a lot, making him much stronger in his role.

## Important Stats

Most of the time, Ogier will be in a Mono Chaos team, so you’ll want to build :realm-mastery: Realm Mastery on him.

:sigil-yield: Sigil Yield boosts the STR generated by Ogier’s exalt, and may be worth building if you have a highly invested Ogier.
