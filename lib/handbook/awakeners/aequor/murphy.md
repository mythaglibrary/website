Murphy is a [keyflare support](/handbook/team#the-almighty-keyflare-bot) who also function as a powerful tank.

Her exalt converts half of incoming damage into Sacrifice, a damage-over-time effect. You can then use her Vortex Reversal skill, or other sources of shielding, to mitigate the damage. In this way, Murphy can help you survive massive attacks, and provides a powerful defensive cooldown for [stall teams](/handbook/team#stall).

Vortex Reversal also inflicts weakness and vulnerable, although Murphy isn’t the ideal character to apply it, as it costs 3 arithmetica to play.

## How to Play

When you’re about to take a lot of damage, use Murphy’s exalt to mitigate it.

Use Vortex Reversal to reduce the Sacrifice on yourself. At E1, it gains retain, so you can reliably have it when you need it.

Murphy’s other cards are also decent. If she’s your [keyflare bot](/handbook/team#the-almighty-keyflare-bot), you should play her Strike and Defense to generate keyflare.

## Teambuilding

Murphy is a defensive support who can be slotted into any team that needs help surviving.

At endgame, she’s often seen in [Vortice](/handbook/awakeners/vortice/) or [Corposant](/handbook/awakeners/vortice/) teams, filling the role of [keyflare support](/handbook/team#the-almighty-keyflare-bot) and tank.

While she isn’t often used with [GMurphy](/handbook/awakeners/gmurphy/), because she doesn’t count as a Lemurian for her [Astral Reign](/handbook/storylines#astral-reign-arc-2) soulforge talent, Murphy has unique synergy with her Genesis counterpart, as she can clear her self-inflicted Sacrifice.

## Important Stats

Build :skey: Keyflare Regen on Murphy if you’re using her as your [keyflare support](/handbook/team#the-almighty-keyflare-bot).

She otherwise has no particular stat requirements and can be given any supportive build you want.
