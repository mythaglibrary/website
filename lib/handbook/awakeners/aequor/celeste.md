Celeste's exalt revives you from death once per fight (once every 6 turns at E3). This means that if you have a glass cannon DPS in danger of dying to normal mob fights, Celeste can carry your team all the way to the boss.

She also inflicts weakness and generates a bit of arithmetica and healing, and her skills can be retained so they don't go back into the deck.
