Celeste is a defensive support who gives you extra lives.

Once per fight, or once every 6 turns at E3, Celeste’s exalt creates a special buff. If you die while this buff is active, you revive and heal HP, consuming the buff in the process.

This revive ability makes Celeste one of the best defensive supports, as each revive effectively tanks one turn of damage no matter how high the difficulty. She's especially good at carrying squishy DPS, like [GHelot](/handbook/awakeners/ghelot/), by reviving once per fight.

In addition to this, Celeste generates a small amount of arithmetica and healing, and she can inflict weakness if needed.

## How to Play

Use Celeste’s exalt to not die.

You can play her cards if you need the arithmetica, weakness, or healing. If you don’t, you can just retain her skills in hand.

## Teambuilding

Celeste is most useful in a team where you know that you’re going to use her revive a lot.

For example, if you have a squishy Caro DPS in danger of dying to normal enemies, like [GHelot](/handbook/awakeners/ghelot/), [Sorel](/handbook/awakeners/sorel/), or [Uvhash](/handbook/awakeners/uvhash/), Celeste can buy an extra turn with her revive.

Alternatively, if you're trying to [stall](/handbook/team#stall) a difficult stage with a grindy DPS like [Vortice](/handbook/awakeners/vortice/), and you need to somehow survive 20+ turns without dying, E3 Celeste may be the safety net you need to clear.

## High Investment

Celeste’s OE puts three Tintless Dreams in hand, which is a lot of arithmetica. Since you don’t need to use Celeste’s exalt often, and you can easily save for her OE, this is effectively a straight upgrade to her exalt.
