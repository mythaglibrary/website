Miryam is an Aequor support who needs to be roused, but in exchange, does pretty much everything.

Her main role is as an aliemus battery and debuffer. She generates aliemus with The Chosen One and her exalt, while providing weakness and vulnerable with her rouse.

In addition, she’s a solid healer; she generates permanent tentacles; she provides STR and Crit DMG buffs; and she can apply and trigger poison for poison teams.

### Sacrament, Obsession, & Ritual

Whenever Miryam exalts, a **Sacrament** is added to your hand. This card can be played to heal for 10% of your missing HP.

If you use any of Miryam’s command cards while you have Sacraments in hand, up to three of those Sacraments are converted into **Obsession.** The command card gets a bonus effect for each Sacrament converted, which is listed on the card as **Ritual.**

Playing Obsession gives Miryam 15 aliemus. If you don’t play it, it will turn back into Sacrament at the start of your next turn. You draw one fewer card at the start of the turn for each Obsession converted.

Most of the time, you can completely ignore this mechanic, and just use Sacrament/Obsession as a source of free healing or aliemus. The bonus effects you get from stockpiling Sacraments are rarely worth giving up so many card draws.

### Lemurians

In [Astral Reign](/handbook/storylines#astral-reign-arc-2) stages, the Lemurian characters get huge buffs from their soulforge talents when used together. These are [Faros](/handbook/awakeners/faros/), [Goliath](/handbook/awakeners/goliath/), [Miryam](/handbook/awakeners/miryam/), [Tulu](/handbook/awakeners/tulu/), and [GMurphy](/handbook/awakeners/gmurphy/).

Miryam is a core support for Lemurian teams, as she provides much-needed aliemus, weakness, and vulnerable.

### DPS Build

If you build Crit DMG on Miryam and/or have her E3, she applies a respectable amount of poison with Exalted Pyre. She isn’t a good solo DPS, but she can pull her weight in poison teams.

## How to Play

Rouse Miryam. Use her exalt in whichever stance is most helpful. Play her cards for aliemus, healing, and various buffs.

## Teambuilding

Miryam is a very flexible support. She fits into any team where her rouse requirement and Aequor realm aren’t an issue.

In D-Effect Zone, she’s almost always used with [GMurphy](/handbook/awakeners/gmurphy/), because she’s required for the [Astral Reign](/handbook/storylines#astral-reign-arc-2) Lemurian team to work.

Miryam can also be a battery for [OE Sanga](/handbook/awakeners/sanga/), [Blue 24](/handbook/awakeners/24/), or [Vortice](/handbook/awakeners/vortice/) teams; or she can form a poison team with characters like [Xu](/handbook/awakeners/xu/) and [Faros](/handbook/awakeners/faros/).

## Important Stats

Miryam’s exalt gets a significant boost from :crit-dmg: Crit DMG, and you want the whole team holding crit stats anyway since you’re running Aequor. This makes Miryam a good holder for supportive Crit DMG wheels like [Power of the Pious](https://skeydb.com/database/wheels/power-of-the-pious), [Private Afternoon](https://skeydb.com/database/wheels/private-afternoon), or [To My Dearest Friend](https://skeydb.com/database/wheels/to-my-dearest-friend).

:crit-dmg: Crit DMG is also the best stat for DPS Miryam. She doesn’t need :crit-rate: Crit Rate since she gets 100% crit from her rouse.

:dmg-amp: DMG Amplification is worse than crit for DPS Miryam, but still useful; you can put it on a teammate like [Faros](/handbook/awakeners/faros/) instead.
