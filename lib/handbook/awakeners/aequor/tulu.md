Tulu is a dedicated support for boosting your Tentacle DMG. He grows your tentacles and provides a crit buff, so your main DPS can deal more damage in Raging Waves stance.

At E1, Tulu can deal AOE damage to all enemies with Abyss Order, which makes him a decent secondary DPS for clearing mobs.

### Lemurians

In [Astral Reign](/handbook/storylines#astral-reign-arc-2) stages, the Lemurian characters get huge buffs from their soulforge talents when used together. These are [Faros](/handbook/awakeners/faros/), [Goliath](/handbook/awakeners/goliath/), [Miryam](/handbook/awakeners/miryam/), [Tulu](/handbook/awakeners/tulu/), and [GMurphy](/handbook/awakeners/gmurphy/).

E1 Tulu is a good secondary DPS for supporting [GMurphy](/handbook/awakeners/gmurphy/), as she has trouble dealing with multiple enemies at once. Tulu also contributes Tentacle DMG, and his soulforge talent grants a unique damage multiplier.

## How to Play

On your burst damage turn, use Tulu’s exalt for a Tentacle DMG and crit buff.

If you have E1 Tulu, Abyss Order is useful for clearing mobs with AOE damage. His cards are otherwise not worth playing and can safely be ignored.

## Teambuilding

Tulu supports damage dealers that rely on triggering tentacle hits, such as [GMurphy](/handbook/awakeners/gmurphy/), [Corposant](/handbook/awakeners/corposant/), and [Aurita](/handbook/awakeners/aurita/).

## Important Stats

:aliemus-regen: Aliemus Regen boosts the STR multiplier of Abyss Order. For E1 Tulu, this is a significant boost to his AOE damage. Unfortunately, Aliemus Regen is a pretty useless stat outside of this, so whether it’s worth building on Tulu depends on how badly you need that extra damage.

For new players who don’t have much Crit Rate, building Crit Rate will probably boost your damage more than building Aliemus Regen, as it means that your tentacles will crit more often.

If you’re a highly invested player and you already have 100% crit chance on your tentacles, building Aliemus Regen on Tulu may be worth it to improve his mobbing capabilities.
