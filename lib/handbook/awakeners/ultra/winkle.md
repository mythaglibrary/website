Winkle is an aliemus support with a variety of useful tools.

In addition to aliemus, she generates keyflare and a bit of shielding, and can inflict weakness.

At E3, Winkle’s exalt lets a teammate exalt twice in one turn, which enables shenanigans like activating [Blade of the Titan](https://skeydb.com/database/wheels/blade-of-the-titan){target="_blank"}’s effect twice.

## How to Play

Use Winkle’s exalt to generate aliemus for her teammates.

Play Energy Beam to inflict weakness, and generate aliemus or keyflare, depending on what you need more.

If your Winkle is level 90, Psyche Reforged generates a decent amount of shield. If you’re a mere mortal with a level 60 Winkle, Psyche Reforged is not terribly useful and can be sold.

## Teambuilding

Winkle is a decent generic support who works as 4th slot filler in most teams.

She works best in teams that are very exalt-reliant, where Winkle’s aliemus helps the team kill enemies faster. For example, she works well with exalt-based DPS like [Daffodil](/handbook/awakeners/daffodil/), [Jenkin](/handbook/awakeners/jenkin/), and [Sanga](/handbook/awakeners/sanga/).

## High Investment

Winkle’s OE generates even more aliemus, although in practice it isn’t used that often. The ability to store excess aliemus is also helpful.
