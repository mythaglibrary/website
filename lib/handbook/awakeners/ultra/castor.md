Castor is a defensive support who generates shields by retaining cards in hand. He also contributes card draw and a source of weakness.

His Onyx Plumes inflict corrosion when played, boosting the damage dealt by Castor’s teammates. At E3, he deals a decent amount of damage himself.

### Onyx Plumes

If you play a Castor card as the first card of the turn, you trigger its **Quasar**. All of his cards have the same Quasar effect, which is to make a shield and add an **Onyx Plume** to your Draw Pile.

Onyx Plumes are 0-cost cards which you can retain in hand to get shields at the end of each turn. If you have teammates that generate alert, such as [Agrippa](/handbook/awakeners/agrippa/) or [Erica](/handbook/awakeners/erica/), they can buff each Onyx Plume individually, resulting in a big shielding boost.

If you don’t need the shields or your hand is too full, you can play Onyx Plumes to deal damage and draw cards. The first Onyx Plume you play each turn inflicts corrosion on all enemies. If you have Castor’s E3, every third Onyx Plume will play will also give you 1 arithmetica.

### Exploration Scaling

For every completed fight, Castor inflicts bonus corrosion for the rest of the exploration. This makes him extra powerful in long stages.

## How to Play

Play one of Castor’s cards as the first card each turn, to trigger his Quasar and accumulate Onyx Plumes in hand.

Retain Onyx Plumes so you get shields every turn. Alternatively, play them to deal damage and draw cards.

## Teambuilding

Castor’s best and most synergistic teammate is his brother [Pollux](/handbook/awakeners/pollux/). A team with these two is referred to as the “bird bros.”

He also fits into many other teams as a defensive support, and can be used with [Jenkin](/handbook/awakeners/jenkin/), [Sanga](/handbook/awakeners/sanga/), [Daffodil](/handbook/awakeners/daffodil/), etc.

His main issue is that he needs to retain a lot of cards in hand, so he doesn’t play well with characters that fight for hand space, like [Kathigu-Ra](/handbook/awakeners/kathigu-ra/) or [GHelot](/handbook/awakeners/ghelot/).

Characters that generate alert, like [Agrippa](/handbook/awakeners/agrippa/) and [Erica](/handbook/awakeners/erica/), work very well with Castor’s Onyx Plumes. Since these characters usually aren’t in demand anywhere else, this helps you make more teams for [D-Effect Zone](/handbook/d-zone).

## High Investment

Castor’s OE is a nice defensive boost if you can afford it. His AA improves his corrosion, making mob fights easier.

## Important Stats

:dmg-amp: DMG Amplification makes Castor inflict more corrosion. Aside from Shield Generation, this is the only stat that really improves his supportive capabilities.

If you’re running DPS Castor, you can ignore :crit-rate: Crit Rate and focus on :crit-dmg: Crit DMG, since his rouse makes his damage always crit.
