Vivian is a flexible support who contributes corrosion, aliemus, STR, and healing. At E1, she also provides a Crit Rate buff.

She grants access to the Primordia: Chaos realm, which gives keyflare at the start of each fight, and is very powerful in [Astral Reign](/handbook/storylines#astral-reign-arc-2) stages.

### The Almighty Soup

Soup Rains from Above inflicts a lot of AOE corrosion. At low levels, this is enough to carry any random DPS through story mode, making Vivian a great support for new players to pull.

Vivian's SSR wheel, [The Seer's Riddle](https://skeydb.com/database/wheels/the-seers-riddle), and her event wheel, [She and her Tree Hole](https://skeydb.com/database/wheels/she-and-her-tree-hole), both boost her corrosion significantly. They’re the best wheels for Vivian if you can get them.

### Quality Nap

Vivian is a very eepy princess. After using her exalt or either of her skills, she will take a **Quality Nap,** sealing her own cards and exalt.

What this means, in practice, is that Vivian can only do one thing each turn. When you play with Vivian, you have to decide which you need the most each turn: corrosion, aliemus, *or* healing.

### Concoct, Cauldron, & Boiling Anger

When you play Whatever’s at Hand for the first time, it will add a **Cauldron** to your hand. This is a buff that must be “cooked” for multiple turns before you can play it. It will be retained across turns and across battles.

You can choose the type of potion that the Cauldron will brew: arithmetica, card draw, or aliemus. At E3, it also multiplies enemy damage taken, a very powerful effect.

If you play Whatever’s at Hand while a Cauldron is in your hand, it increases the cooking time, but it also makes the potion buff stronger (up to 2 extra times). It also adds **Boiling Anger**, which prevents Vivian from taking a nap, so you can use more than one of her abilities in the same turn.

If you play Soup Rains from Above while a Cauldron is in your hand, it speeds up the countdown of the Cauldron by 1 turn.

You can use the Cauldron more than once per exploration, but you can’t pick the same potion again until you’ve exhausted all three options.

## How to Play

### Single Cauldron Strategy

The easiest way to play Vivian is to ignore her Cauldron, and just treat her as a generic support with useful abilities. Simply play her cards, or use her exalt, according to the situation.

As you do this, the Cauldron will cook throughout the exploration, and be ready in time for the boss fight. Then you can play it as a powerful one-time buff, and use Boiling Anger and Vivian’s cards to set up a burst turn for your DPS.

Depending on your team, you’ll probably want either the arithmetica potion or the aliemus potion. The card draw potion is of limited use, since you can easily draw cards with the Primordia: Chaos triple posse.

### Multiple Cauldron Strategy

You can use the Cauldron more than once during a stage. However, this requires you to carefully manage your Cauldron countdown, Vivian’s skills, and her remaining potions.

Ideally, you use the 2 weakest potions during the exploration, and enter the boss fight with the third potion already prepared in your Cauldron. This lets you use that Cauldron to beat the first phase of the boss, and immediately start cooking a new Cauldron with any potion of your choice.

## Teambuilding

Vivian fits into most teams, since her two main abilities, corrosion and aliemus, are universally useful.

If you're a new player doing story mode, putting Vivian with any AOE damage dealer, such as [Sanga](/handbook/awakeners/sanga/) or [Jenkin](/handbook/awakeners/jenkin/), is enough to make a functional team.

Vivian's best teammates are those that want to rouse early, deal a lot of damage in one turn, and aren’t picky about what posse they get. DPS that function this way include [Mouchette](/handbook/awakeners/mouchette/), [Daffodil](/handbook/awakeners/daffodil/), and DPS [GOgier](/handbook/awakeners/gogier/).

In contrast, due to Vivian’s Primordia: Chaos realm, she doesn’t work as well with DPS that want to spam a specific posse, like [Vortice](/handbook/awakeners/vortice/) or [Kathigu-Ra](/handbook/awakeners/kathigu-ra/). (But [Kathigu-Ra](/handbook/awakeners/kathigu-ra/) benefits enough from Vivian’s support that they work decently together anyway.)

## Investment

All of Vivian’s enlightens are quite good. E3 in particular is a power spike, as it gives her a +30% damage buff which is multiplicative with other buffs.

Vivian’s OE makes her a good healer. Her AA is very powerful since it effectively provides extra arithmetica each turn.

## Important Stats

:crit-rate: Crit Rate increases the likelihood of getting extra healing from Vivian’s exalt. At E1, it also boosts her corrosion and the Crit Rate buff she grants.
