A supportive DPS who steadily blasts enemies with pursuits while boosting your Tentacle DMG and Tranquil Sea shields.

Her exalt takes time to set up, but deals huge AOE damage once prepared, especially if she has E3 and her wheel.

Needs her posse, Reload! skill levels, and soulforge levels. She likes defensive teammates who can stall while she's ramping up.
